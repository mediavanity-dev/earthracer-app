EarthRacer · drive the real Earth, for iPhone

Support

EarthRacer is an open-world driving game built live from open map data: real streets, real buildings, real weather and real speed limits, anywhere on Earth. Questions or problems? Email mediavanity@gmail.com.

Getting started

Pick a starting point: where you are, a search, or one of the suggested places. After that you always carry on from exactly where you stopped. To go somewhere new, drive there, take a bus, train, ferry or plane from a real stop (in real time), go through a portal, or use one of your four random placements a week.

Open ☰ for the Garage (vehicles, repairs), today's delivery jobs and race, your atlas, feats and settings.

Common questions

How do I steer?

On iPhone choose Tilt (hold the phone like a wheel; tap the wheel dial at the bottom to re-centre), Slider or Buttons, in Settings. On a computer use W A S D or the arrow keys, Space or Shift for the handbrake, or a game controller.

The map is slow to appear, or I'm held back to a lower speed

EarthRacer loads the map around you as you drive. If it can't load fast enough it holds your speed until it catches up ("map loading ahead"). Check your connection; it carries on by itself.

What's my top speed?

Each vehicle goes at its real-world speed. On roads where OpenStreetMap knows the speed limit you can go up to 10 mph (16 km/h) over it; elsewhere vehicles are held to 80 km/h for now.

I've run out of fuel

Stop, and the game offers a tow truck (for coins), another of your vehicles, the emergency solar buggy in daylight, or siphoning fuel from another vehicle at night. Fuel stations (⛽ on the map) are free.

What's a portal?

Once an hour, for ten minutes, a portal opens somewhere in your town. A glowing star on the edge of the map points the way. Drive through for lots of points and a one-way trip to the city on its label.

Explorer or Gamer mode?

Explorer is the real world with a few stunts to find. Gamer adds ramps, coins, boost arrows, cones, signs to knock down and trees to topple. Switch any time in Settings.

Keeping my progress

Your game is saved on your phone. Start over (in Settings) deletes it for good.

Tips

EarthRacer is free, with no ads and nothing to unlock. The optional tips at the bottom of Settings unlock nothing; they help keep it going. Apple handles the payment.
