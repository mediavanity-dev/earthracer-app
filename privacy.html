EarthRacer · drive the real Earth, for iPhone and Android

Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

EarthRacer collects no data about you. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing about you or where you drive is sent to us.

Your location

If you choose "Start where I am", EarthRacer asks for your location once, to put your starting point on the map. It isn't tracked after that: in the game you drive a virtual vehicle, and your real position isn't used again.

The map and the world around your vehicle

To build the world around your vehicle, the app asks public services for data about the area it's in, the way a map app does: map tiles (OpenFreeMap), building detail and speed limits (OpenStreetMap, via Overpass), terrain (AWS), weather and waves (Open-Meteo), place names (Nominatim), postcards of landmarks (Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons) and a list of radio stations for the country (Radio Browser). Radio plays directly from the station you choose. If you open street view, the app asks Mapillary for street photos near where you are; if you choose “Open Google Street View”, Google Maps opens at that spot, under Google’s own privacy policy. These requests carry the area, not who you are, and we receive none of them.

Motion

With tilt steering, the phone's motion sensor turns your vehicle. The readings stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your game (your spot on Earth, vehicles, coins, atlas, postcards, race times and settings) is saved only on your phone. Deleting the app, or Start over in Settings, deletes it.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store on iPhone, or Google Play on Android. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account or Google account information.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply. We don't add you to any list or share your email.

Children

EarthRacer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
